About BusTrackerAI

BusTrackerAI is a web-based AI-enabled bus tracking and ticket booking system for two roles: passengers and drivers.

Passengers search buses, view routes, track buses live, select seats, book tickets, manage bookings and get intelligent travel info. Drivers log in, view assigned bus/route, start/end trips and share live location. The Flask backend exposes REST APIs, MongoDB stores users/buses/routes/bookings/trips/locations, and rule-based AI prototypes provide ETA prediction and route recommendation (ML-ready architecture).
